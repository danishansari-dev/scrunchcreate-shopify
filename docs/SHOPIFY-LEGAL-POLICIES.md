## 1. Purchase Options Cancellation Policy (Required ⚠️)

> **Why this is required:** Shopify flags this as **Required** when subscriptions, pre-orders, or purchase option apps are present on the store. Pasting this policy resolves the warning and ensures full checkout compliance.

### Copy & Paste Text:

```text
PURCHASE OPTIONS & SUBSCRIPTION CANCELLATION POLICY

At Scrunch & Create (scrunchcreate.com), we strive to provide complete flexibility and transparency for all purchase options, pre-orders, and recurring deliveries.

1. Cancellation Before Dispatch
You may cancel any standard order or pre-order purchase option at any time before it has been dispatched from our studio (typically within 12 hours of placing the order). Once an order has been handed over to our courier partner and a tracking AWB has been generated, it cannot be cancelled in transit.

2. Recurring Subscriptions & Purchase Options
If you enroll in any recurring subscription box, scheduled gift delivery, or recurring purchase option:
- Cancel Anytime: You can cancel, pause, or adjust your recurring subscription at any time with zero penalty or cancellation fees.
- Notice Period: Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours prior to your scheduled recurring billing/shipment date.
- How to Cancel: You can manage your subscription directly by logging into your Scrunch & Create Customer Account, or by sending a cancellation request with your Order/Subscription ID to our WhatsApp Support (+91 73009 69491) or Email (scrunchandcreate@gmail.com).
- Billing After Cancellation: Once cancelled, you will not be billed for any future billing cycles. Any order that has already been billed and dispatched prior to cancellation will be fulfilled as scheduled.

3. Pre-Orders & Custom Hampers
For personalized or custom curated hampers ordered under a purchase option, cancellation is permitted only prior to the start of custom assembly/packaging.

4. Contact Support
If you have any questions regarding your purchase options or need assistance cancelling a recurring order, our team is available:
- WhatsApp: +91 73009 69491 (Mon–Sat, 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM IST)
- Email: scrunchandcreate@gmail.com
```

```text
RETURN, REPLACEMENT & REFUND POLICY

At Scrunch & Create, every scrunchie, hair bow, hair accessory, and curated gift hamper is handcrafted with the highest quality materials and artisanal attention to detail.

1. Hygiene & Safety Notice
Due to strict hygiene, health, and personal safety standards governing hair and personal accessories, opened or used items cannot be returned or exchanged. We accept returns and replacements exclusively for items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect.

2. 5-Day Replacement & Return Window
If you receive an item that is defective, damaged in transit, or different from what you ordered, you are entitled to a free replacement or full refund:
- Reporting Timeline: You must notify us within 5 days of delivery.
- Condition: The item must be unused, unwashed, and in its original packaging with all tags attached.
- Mandatory Unboxing Video: To protect both our customers and our artisanal studio against courier tampering or transit damage, a clear unboxing video recorded continuously from the moment the sealed courier parcel is opened is MANDATORY for all damage or missing item claims.

3. How to Submit a Claim
Initiating a return or replacement is simple:
1. Contact us via WhatsApp at +91 73009 69491 or email scrunchandcreate@gmail.com.
2. Share your Order Number (e.g. #1001), continuous unboxing video, and clear photos highlighting the defect or damage.
3. Our customer support team will verify your request within 24 business hours.
4. Upon approval, we will arrange a reverse courier pickup or immediately dispatch a brand-new replacement at zero cost to you.

4. Refund Timelines & Method
If a replacement is unavailable or a refund is approved:
- Prepaid Orders: The refund is initiated to your original payment method (UPI, Bank Account, or Card) within 24–48 hours of inspection approval. The amount typically reflects in your account within 5–7 business days depending on your bank.
- Store Credit: You may also opt for an instant Scrunch & Create digital store gift card or discount voucher.

5. Order Cancellations
Orders may be cancelled within 12 hours of placement or prior to dispatch. Once an order is handed over to the courier partner with a generated tracking ID, cancellation is not possible.
```

```text
Thank you for choosing Scrunch & Create! We take immense pride in handcrafting, inspecting, and packaging each order with loving care.

1. Free Shipping & Delivery Charges
- Free Standard Shipping: Available on all prepaid domestic orders of ₹499 and above across India.

https://scrunchcreate.com/pages/track-order

If you notice that the outer parcel is severely damaged or tampered with at the time of delivery, please do not accept the package or record an unboxing video immediately upon opening, and contact our WhatsApp support team at +91 73009 69491 within 24 hours.
```

## How to Apply These in Shopify Admin (Step-by-Step)

1. Stay on your current screen: **Shopify Admin > Settings > Policies** (`admin.shopify.com/store/scrunchcreate/settings/legal`).
2. Click **Purchase options cancellation policy** (the one marked with the orange **Required** tag).
3. Paste the text from **Section 1** above into the editor box, then click **Save**.
4. Click **Return and refund policy**, paste the text from **Section 2**, then click **Save**.
5. Click **Shipping policy**, paste the text from **Section 3**, then click **Save**.
6. Click **Terms of service**, paste the text from **Section 4**, then click **Save**.
7. Click **Contact information**, paste the text from **Section 5**, then click **Save**.
8. Once saved, refresh the page: all warnings will disappear, and your checkout footer will automatically link to these official policies!
